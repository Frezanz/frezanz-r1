# Time — Comparisons

## Terms that may overlap but must not be silently equated

| Project phrase | Possible established concept | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| measure of change | parameterization, duration, clock time | measurement ≠ ontology |
| object's timeline | worldline, history, state trajectory | “timeline” is broader/less precise |
| unique time position | spacetime event/coordinate, proper time | coordinate ≠ intrinsic identity |
| time differs by object | proper time / reference-frame dependence | can confuse frame dependence with separate fundamental times |
| time is perception | psychological time | too broad if applied to physical time |
| change is unique | path/history/state trajectory | “unique” needs an identity criterion |
