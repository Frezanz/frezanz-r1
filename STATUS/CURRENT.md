# Current State

## Active research direction

The project is currently interrogating the concept **Time** as a candidate fundamental concept.

## Working position

The user proposes a model in which:
- time is related to measurement of change;
- entities have histories/trajectories;
- change and uniqueness may depend on state, position, history, and interaction;
- conventional clock time may not capture the intended underlying concept;
- perspective/reference may matter.

These are project statements, not established facts.

## Current methodological priority

Separate:
1. change itself,
2. observation of change,
3. measurement of change,
4. temporal ordering,
5. duration,
6. reference-frame dependence,
7. psychological perception of time,
8. object identity/history.

## Next step

Continue adversarial interrogation of **Time** before producing a foundational formula.
