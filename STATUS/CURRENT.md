# Current State

## Active research directions

1. Continue interrogating **Time** as a candidate fundamental concept.
2. Begin documenting a **formula-network intelligence architecture**: a common runtime coordinating typed executable formulae, selectively activated paths, structured context, and learned routing.
3. Treat a **concentration engine** as a proposed supervisory process for error diagnosis, structural optimization, context organization, and compute reduction under benchmark and rollback controls.

## Working position

The user proposes that:
- fundamental representations may include characters, quantities, units, symbols, relations, states, and unknowns;
- formulae are independently executable units that can interact through typed dependencies and shared state;
- reasoning paths can be constructed and improved from predictions, evaluation, and failure analysis;
- natural language should be a learned interpretation/output layer, not necessarily the foundation of every computation;
- context should be organized by relation/category with source, confidence, and explicit time metadata;
- user-customizable formulae, filters, formats, capabilities, and workflows should share a common engine;
- a concentration/maintenance process should inspect traces and propose optimizations during an explicit idle mode.

These are project hypotheses and design proposals, not established results.

## Methodological priority

- Keep deterministic formula execution separate from uncertain selection and language generation.
- Start with a small, testable engine before implementing all proposed layers.
- Measure generalization, error recovery, resource use, and reliability against baselines.
- Preserve provenance, timestamps, user permissions, and rollback for memory/code/architecture changes.

## Next step

Prototype a typed formula registry and directed execution graph with 5–10 units; trace activations and verify outputs before adding adaptive routing.

For the ongoing Time investigation, keep separating change, observation, measurement, temporal order, duration, reference, perceived time, and entity identity/history before committing to a foundational formula.
