# Time — Hypotheses

## H1
Time may be more fundamentally describable as a measure/ordering associated with change than as an independently flowing substance.

Status: hypothesis.

## H2
An object's “timeline” may be better represented as its history/state trajectory than as a separate object-specific time substance.

Status: hypothesis.

## H3
The experienced or measured value of time may depend on the chosen reference/system, while some deeper invariant structure may remain.

Status: hypothesis compatible with known relativity at a broad level, but the project-specific interpretation remains unformalized.

## H4
Uniqueness may arise from the combination of intrinsic properties and relations involving state, location, history, and interactions rather than from time and position alone.

Status: hypothesis.

## H5
“Time is perception-based” may be too broad; the project may need separate concepts for psychological time, measurement/reference dependence, and physical temporal structure.

Status: hypothesis / definition target.
